# A diversification has a progenitor, a clone and a child

A new harness is made in two steps, and each step has a word of its own:

- A cloning is `cladenc` copying the source code of a harness. The copy is the clone.
- A diversification is a harness changing a clone, in a container.

Three harnesses are part of the two steps:

- The progenitor is the harness that is cloned. `cladenc` starts it in a container for the diversification, and it is never changed.
- The progenitor's clone is the copy of the progenitor's source code that the progenitor is asked to improve. It is all the progenitor changes. Where the primogenitor is the one that is cloned, it is the primogenitor's clone.
- The child is the new harness that the progenitor's clone becomes when the progenitor has ended.

The first harness that is cloned in a directed evolution is the primogenitor, as no other harness exists yet. From the second diversification on, the harness that `cladenc` chooses is the progenitor, and it can be the primogenitor again.

In an experiment the progenitor is cloned a second time, and the primogenitor diversifies the second clone. That is a cloning and a diversification as well, per [[the-singleton-control-group-harness]].

**Why:** The three are different things with different roles, and each has a name of its own. Until 2026-11-25 one word, procreation, covered both steps, though `cladenc` carries out the first and a harness the second. On that day the user split the word in two and dropped it.

**How to apply:** These are the names, in the code and everywhere else. A harness is cloned, and it diversifies its clone; procreation, procreate and their other forms are not said. Diversification is the word wherever a container, a time limit, an outcome or a count is meant, as in `--max-diversifications`, since a cloning is only a copy that `cladenc` makes. The tests of the older formats of the journal keep the word those formats had. Related: [[the-first-harness-is-the-primogenitor]], [[the-paper-is-a-starting-point]].
