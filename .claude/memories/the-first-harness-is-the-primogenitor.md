# The first harness is the primogenitor

The harness a directed evolution starts from is the primogenitor. It is not called the progenitor. A progenitor is any harness that has a child, and the primogenitor is the first of them: the one harness that is no child.

**Why:** It is the user's rule. The two words name two things, and with progenitor used for the first harness a reader cannot tell whether a sentence is about the first harness or about the parent of the procreation at hand.

**How to apply:** Say primogenitor in replies, issues, commits, the README, the help, what `cladenc` prints and the names in the code, the flag `--primogenitor` among them. Never write progenitor for the first harness.

Mine, not the user's: parent stays the word for the harness that procreates in one procreation, per [[a-procreation-has-a-parent-a-copy-and-a-child]], and progenitor is left without a use until the user gives it one.
