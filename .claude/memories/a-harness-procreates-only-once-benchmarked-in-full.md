# A harness procreates only once benchmarked in full

A harness can be the progenitor of a procreation only once it has been benchmarked in full: it has attempted every task of every benchmark the directed evolution was given, and each of those attempts has finished. Where a procreation is due and no harness has been benchmarked in full, an attempt is started in its place.

**Why:** It is the user's rule. To the user a harness is benchmarked against its benchmarks, each as a whole, and a progenitor learns from all of that. That each harness takes as many attempts as there are tasks is known to the user and accepted. My reading, not the user's words: a harness that has attempted part of its tasks does not count as benchmarked yet.

**How to apply:**

- Do not propose one task, another number of tasks, or a flag that gives the number, as what a harness needs before it procreates.
- The rule is about who may procreate. That every harness is benchmarked in full, whether it procreates or not, and that the number a person gives is the most procreations, is [[every-harness-is-benchmarked-in-full]].
- Where a harness that has attempted one task or some of the tasks is meant, say how many tasks it has attempted, per [[an-explanation-says-what-happens]], in the words of [[the-words-about-benchmarks]].
