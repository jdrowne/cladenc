# The singleton control group harness

`cladenc` conducts an experiment to test whether a progenitor is better at diversifying clones into better harnesses than the primogenitor. To conduct the experiment, `cladenc` has to establish a baseline. `cladenc` establishes the baseline by cloning the progenitor a second time and having the primogenitor diversify the second clone. The harness the primogenitor creates by diversifying the second clone of the progenitor is the singleton control group harness.

**Why:** The experiment has to be worded with care. Each word says what the thing is for: an experiment tests something, a baseline is what an experiment needs, and a control group is what an experiment compares against. A short form that names none of it, such as the primogenitor making a control, is not used.

**How to apply:** In the README, an issue, a commit, a reply and the names in the code, say that `cladenc` conducts the experiment and establishes the baseline, and that the primogenitor creates the singleton control group harness. Write the name in full each time. What the primogenitor does there is a diversification, and the second copy of the progenitor a cloning, as the user held on 2026-11-25: the progenitor is cloned twice, and the progenitor diversifies one clone and the primogenitor the other. The diversification of an experiment does not count toward `--max-diversifications`, and the singleton control group harness is nobody's child. The progenitor still diversifies its own clone into the progenitor's child, as in [[a-diversification-has-a-progenitor-a-clone-and-a-child]]. Issue 72 holds the design, in the words it had when it was closed.
